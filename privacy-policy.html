How Taurux Group collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through this website.

Last updated: June 2026

Taurux Group (“Taurux”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information submitted through tauruxgroup.com, including information provided through our contact form.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us through the website, including:

  • full name
  • company name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • inquiry type
  • message content

We may also collect limited technical information automatically when you browse the website, such as browser type, device type, IP address, and basic usage data.

2. Why We Collect Your Information

We collect personal information for legitimate business purposes, including responding to inquiries, evaluating potential partnerships or business opportunities, communicating with you regarding your request, maintaining appropriate business records, and improving the website and user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information will be used only for the purposes for which it was collected, or for purposes compatible with those purposes, including follow-up communications related to your inquiry. We do not sell personal information.

4. Limiting Collection

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes identified in this Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website or manage communications, such as website hosting providers, form handling or email delivery providers, analytics providers, or technical support providers. These service providers are expected to handle information only for authorized purposes.

6. Protection of Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain appropriate business records.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, withdraw consent where applicable, or request deletion of information where legally permitted.

9. Cookies and Technical Data

This website may use standard technical tools, such as cookies or analytics tools, to improve performance, security, and user experience. These tools may collect technical information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, browser type, and device data.
